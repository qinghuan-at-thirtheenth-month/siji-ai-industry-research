# Independent Evaluator

The evaluator reviews the public task conditions, tool trace, source provenance, final reports, and preregistered metrics.

Primary question:

> With both agents having the same Web capability, did adding SIJI improve the research process and result in ways supported by observable evidence?

The evaluator specifically checks whether the SIJI-enabled condition changed:

- research depth and continuity;
- structural completeness;
- identity stability;
- relationship-type discipline;
- business-stage accuracy;
- cross-chain reasoning;
- conditional-conclusion quality;
- next-verification actionability;
- Web rediscovery vs new-evidence search;
- source traceability.

The evaluator does **not** reward:

- more SIJI calls by themselves;
- a longer report by itself;
- raw fact count without source quality;
- more nodes without follow-up;
- a more confident conclusion without stronger evidence.

## Case-specific checks

### Demand → Upstream Impact

The evaluator checks whether the report:

- separates the downstream starting fact from upstream inference;
- avoids mixing product/platform generations;
- explains the propagation mechanism and preconditions;
- identifies alternative bottlenecks;
- gives falsifiable next-verification steps.

### Next Inflection → Market Expectation

The evaluator checks whether the report:

- separates industry-ramp evidence from market-pricing evidence;
- resolves product → business → listed-company mapping;
- avoids treating industry evidence as a stock valuation signal;
- states “not established” when comparable market evidence is insufficient;
- does not force a winner.

A valid evaluation can show positive, neutral, mixed, or negative effects from adding SIJI.
