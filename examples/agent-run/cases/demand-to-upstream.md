# Evaluation Task — Demand → Upstream Impact

## User task

I want to understand how an AI rack upgrade can affect upstream products rather than get another list of “related companies.”

Start from public information about the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and research memory and advanced packaging relevant to next-generation AI rack evolution.

First establish what has actually changed. Then research upstream and downstream:

- which product requirements or delivery constraints may change;
- which product generations are relevant;
- which companies have specific product, participation, or commercial-stage evidence;
- which links are only structural or thematic;
- what is the strongest conditional conclusion supported by the evidence;
- what should be checked over the next two quarters to strengthen or overturn that conclusion.

Do not treat product availability as deployment volume. Do not treat platform compatibility as customer procurement. Key conclusions require original sources.

## Required output

- scope and `data_as_of`;
- no more than a few key conditional conclusions;
- evidence-backed propagation path for each conclusion;
- relevant products / companies / positions;
- business-stage timeline;
- unknowns / conflicts / evidence gaps;
- alternative explanations and invalidation conditions;
- concrete next-verification actions;
- source provenance for every key fact and relationship.
