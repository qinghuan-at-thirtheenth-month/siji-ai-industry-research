# Evaluation Task — Next Inflection → Market Expectation

## User task

Research potential next inflection points in AI infrastructure.

For the first scope, compare memory/HBM and advanced-packaging products related to next-generation AI infrastructure over the next 6–12 months.

Do not rank products by recent news volume. Instead:

- identify products with concrete evidence of adoption, qualification, production, shipment, capacity, or changing constraints;
- explain where demand comes from and what dependencies could change the ramp;
- map specific companies to the product/business evidence;
- identify the strongest industry research candidate, if the evidence supports one;
- compare alternative product paths;
- independently examine available market evidence for the related listed companies;
- state whether the evidence supports any conclusion about expectations already being overextended.

Industry outlook and market pricing must be separate conclusions. Product evidence is not a valuation signal. If the market evidence is insufficient or incomparable, say so rather than forcing a winner.

## Required output

- scope, observation window, and `data_as_of`;
- industry candidates and comparison basis;
- conditional industry conclusion;
- product → business → company mapping;
- commercial-stage timeline;
- bottlenecks, alternatives, and invalidation conditions;
- independent market-evidence section;
- separate market-expectation conclusion;
- unknowns / conflicts / evidence gaps;
- concrete next-verification actions;
- original sources and provenance.
