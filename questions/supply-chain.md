# Supply-Chain Research Questions

Use SIJI when the question is about **how products/positions connect and which companies participate**, not when the task is simply “list related stocks.”

Typical questions: What is upstream/downstream of a product? Which companies participate at this position? Is Company A actually a supplier to Company B? Is a relation structural, cooperative, compatible, or a disclosed transaction? Which parallel/alternative routes exist?

Hard boundary:

```text
structural upstream/downstream
≠ disclosed supplier/customer
```

A direct business relation needs its own source, time and scope. SIJI should leave it unknown/review-required rather than promote a structural edge to a transaction.
